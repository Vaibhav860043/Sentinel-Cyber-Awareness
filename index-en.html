Everyday cyber safety

Small habits. Stronger protection.

Scams often rely on urgency, impersonation, or unexpected payment requests. Learn the warning signs and take a moment to verify before acting.

Browse all 15 guides  →
SPOT THE SIGNALS

Check unexpected links, sender details, urgent requests, and offers that seem too good to be true.

PROTECT YOUR ACCOUNTS

Use unique passwords, turn on multi-factor authentication, and keep devices updated.

PAUSE BEFORE PAYING

Never share a UPI PIN or approve a payment request you did not expect.

KNOW WHAT TO DO

Contact your bank quickly, save evidence, and report cyber fraud through official channels.

A safer online routine

Make security part of everyday life.

Pause before you respond to unexpected messages. Verify important requests through a trusted number or official app. Keep recovery options current and report suspicious activity quickly.

Use the guides below to learn one practical safety habit at a time.

CORE HABITS
  • Pause when a message creates urgency
  • Verify people and links independently
  • Use unique passwords and multi-factor authentication
  • Report fraud through official channels
REMEMBER

Your bank, government agency, or support team should never ask you to share your PIN or one-time password.
